Hack Nerd Font bundled privately with Twine

Nerd Fonts version: 3.4.0
Hack version: 3.003
Source: https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/releases/download/v3.4.0/Hack.zip

The four standard HackNerdFont faces (not Mono or Propo) are redistributed
with metadata-only name changes so Twine's private fallback does not shadow
fonts installed by the user. The private family is "Twine Terminal Nerd Font";
PostScript names are TwineHackNF-Regular, -Bold, -Italic, and -BoldItalic.
Glyph outlines, advances, hinting, kerning, character maps, and style flags
are unchanged. Only the naming table and checksum adjustment were modified.

Name-table transformation (FontTools 4.60.1):
- Read upstream faces with TTFont(recalcBBoxes=False, recalcTimestamp=False).
- Change existing records 1, 16, 21 to "Twine Terminal Nerd Font".
- Change record 3 to "3.003;TWNE;TwineHackNF-<style>".
- Change records 4 and 18 to the private family plus the existing subfamily.
- Change record 6 to "TwineHackNF-<style>" and record 25 to "TwineHackNF".
- Preserve all other records, including subfamilies, copyright, and licenses.
- Save with reorderTables=False; verify every table except name/head is
  byte-identical and head differs only at its checksum-adjustment bytes.
